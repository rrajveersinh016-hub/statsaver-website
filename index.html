Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Does the sender know I saved their status?

No. WhatsApp does not notify users when statuses are viewed or saved via local device cache.

Does StatSaver work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. You can enable WhatsApp Business in Settings to view and save business statuses seamlessly.

Where are saved statuses stored?

Permanently saved media is stored in your device's public Pictures/StatSaver (for images) and Movies/StatSaver (for videos) directories.

Is StatSaver safe to use?

Yes, 100%. StatSaver does not require user credentials, uses official Android Storage Access Framework (SAF), and operates strictly offline on your phone.
