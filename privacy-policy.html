Privacy Policy — StatSaver

Last updated: August 2026

StatSaver ("the App") is developed and published by 1010 Technologies ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, stores, and shares when you use it.

1. No Account Required

StatSaver does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal identity information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the App.

2. What the App Accesses On Your Device

With your explicit permission, granted through Android's Storage Access Framework, the App reads WhatsApp's status media folder on your device so it can display statuses within the App. The App also accesses your device's media storage to save files you choose to download.

All status media the App displays, caches, or saves stays on your own device. We do not upload, transmit, or have any server-side access to your photos, videos, or saved statuses.

3. Local Storage

The App stores the following information locally on your device only, using a local database:

  • Records of statuses you have saved (filenames, timestamps, file locations)
  • Your app preferences (language, theme, notification settings, retention period)

None of this local data is transmitted to us or any third party.

4. Analytics & Crash Reporting

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to help us understand app performance and fix crashes. These services may collect:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language settings)
  • App usage events (e.g. a status was saved, a screen was viewed)
  • Crash logs and diagnostic data if the App encounters an error

This data is associated with an anonymous device/installation identifier, not with your name or identity. It is processed by Google in accordance with the Firebase Privacy and Security policy.

5. Advertising

The App may display advertisements served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's advertising policies. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings.

6. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information collected by the App to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Permissions We Request and Why

  • Storage / Media access: to read WhatsApp statuses and save files you choose to download.
  • Notifications: optional, to alert you when new statuses are detected. You can disable this in Settings at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support.1010technologies.apps@gmail.com
